Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding bookings and support.

How do I modify or cancel a booking?

Log in to your BookVerse account, go to "My Bookings", select the ticket or reservation, and click on "Manage / Cancel Booking".

When will I get my refund?

Refunds are processed automatically within 24 hours of cancellation and typically reflect in your bank account within 3–5 business days.

How do I present my e-ticket?

You can show the QR code received on your registered email address or access it directly inside the BookVerse mobile app.
